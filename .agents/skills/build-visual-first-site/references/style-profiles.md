# Style profiles and evidence router

Choose one primary profile from image evidence and page purpose. Offer at most one credible alternative. Mix only one compatible secondary trait.

## Scoring method

Score every plausible profile on four axes from 0 to 2:

1. **Subject fit**
   - 0: the profile conflicts with the main subject.
   - 1: the subject can support the profile with careful framing.
   - 2: the subject is a native fit.
2. **Composition fit**
   - 0: the required crops or text placement would damage the image.
   - 1: the composition works in some viewports or with framing.
   - 2: negative space, geometry, orientation, and crop tolerance strongly support it.
3. **Collection fit**
   - 0: image count, consistency, or sequence contradicts the profile.
   - 1: the set is usable with curation or one missing asset.
   - 2: image count and visual relationships naturally support the profile.
4. **Purpose fit**
   - 0: it weakens the user's goal or primary action.
   - 1: it can serve the goal but is not the clearest route.
   - 2: it directly strengthens the goal and audience experience.

Total each profile out of 8 and retain one sentence of visual evidence per axis. Select the highest score only if it reaches 5. If every profile scores below 5, use a neutral framed composition and request or create a better visual anchor instead of forcing a style.

Tie-break in this order: purpose fit, composition fit, subject fit, collection fit. If still tied, choose the option that protects the image better on mobile. “Beautiful,” “premium,” or “AI-looking” is never evidence.

## Poetic Immersive

Best for panoramic landscapes, seasons, mountains, lakes, coastlines, gardens, and dreamlike travel scenes.

- Native subject evidence: environment or scenery is itself the story.
- Native composition evidence: wide depth, open sky or water, clear safe regions, crop-tolerant atmosphere.
- Native collection evidence: one strong world or a small coherent scenic series.
- Native purpose evidence: evoke place, mood, wonder, or seasonal memory.
- Image role: full-bleed emotional world.
- Layout: sparse overlay anchored to real negative space.
- Type: expressive display face paired with a clean sans serif.
- Motion: breeze, petals, water glints, clouds, birds, leaves, or snow already implied by the scene.
- Avoid: dense navigation, card stacks, hard product UI, or copy covering scenery.

## Quiet Luxury

Best for architecture, restrained products, interiors, galleries, premium hospitality, and controlled photography.

- Native subject evidence: material, object, room, building, or crafted detail.
- Native composition evidence: geometry, clean edges, precise negative space, controlled light.
- Native collection evidence: a small curated set with consistent finish.
- Native purpose evidence: premium presentation, consideration, booking, or product confidence.
- Image role: curated object or space.
- Layout: strong grid, wide margins, small metadata, precise alignment.
- Type: refined sans serif with an optional restrained serif accent.
- Motion: slow reveals, subtle light shifts, and measured material response.
- Avoid: ornate script, noisy particles, excessive transparency, or theatrical claims.

## Travel Editorial

Best for multiple trip photographs, people, city details, documentary moments, and journals.

- Native subject evidence: places plus lived details, people, dates, routes, or observations.
- Native composition evidence: mixed orientations and varied crops benefit from sequencing.
- Native collection evidence: several distinct but related images with useful metadata.
- Native purpose evidence: document, browse, remember, or understand a journey.
- Image role: sequenced visual evidence.
- Layout: asymmetric editorial grid, chapters, dates, locations, captions, and indices.
- Type: magazine-like display plus practical reading text.
- Motion: section reveals, restrained image drift, and progress cues.
- Avoid: treating every image as a hero or hiding useful context.

## Cinematic Story

Best for a deliberate image sequence with emotional progression, scene changes, or before-and-after states.

- Native subject evidence: scenes imply a beginning, transformation, and destination.
- Native composition evidence: individual frames can hold attention in sequence.
- Native collection evidence: order matters more than variety.
- Native purpose evidence: guide the viewer through a narrative or reveal.
- Image role: full-screen scenes or framed shots in narrative order.
- Layout: acts, chapters, pacing, and controlled disclosure.
- Type: bold but economical titles with sparse supporting copy.
- Motion: slow scene transitions, masks, parallax, and camera-like movement.
- Avoid: autoplay audio, rapid cuts, ornamental transitions, or motion without story value.

## Clear Lifestyle

Best for food, home, wellness, personal projects, bright travel, family-safe storytelling, portraits, and approachable brands.

- Native subject evidence: human, tactile, domestic, edible, wearable, or everyday experience.
- Native composition evidence: bright, approachable scenes with readable details.
- Native collection evidence: modular moments work independently or as a light story.
- Native purpose evidence: invite, explain, share, reassure, or make a personal project welcoming.
- Image role: warm evidence of everyday life.
- Layout: open, modular, readable, and lightly editorial.
- Type: clear sans serif with an optional human serif accent.
- Motion: gentle micro-interactions, steam, fabric, light glints, or soft entrance transitions.
- Avoid: corporate density, artificial luxury cues, or childish decoration.

## Bias checks

Before final selection, verify:

- A single landscape did not automatically trigger Poetic Immersive when the purpose is a multi-entry journal.
- The word “premium” did not automatically trigger Quiet Luxury without compositional evidence.
- A large image count did not automatically trigger Travel Editorial when the images form a deliberate cinematic sequence.
- A warm palette did not automatically trigger Clear Lifestyle when the purpose is architectural or product-focused.
- The result does not reproduce a prior seasonal page's centered hero, glass footer, weather control, or typography merely because it worked once.
